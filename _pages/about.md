I am a Graduate Student of Computer Science at [Old Dominion University](https://www.odu.edu/) pursuing my M.S. in Computer Science with the expected graduation date of December 2026. Sponsored by the Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC), I'm attending through the Science, Mathematics, and Research for Transformation ([SMART](https://www.smartscholarship.org/smart)) Scholarship-for-Service Program, as a SMART Scholar. Funded by the Department of Defense (DoD), [SMART](https://www.smartscholarship.org/smart) is a combined educational and workforce development opportunity for STEM related fields.
<br>
<br>
I served in the United State Navy as a Fire Controlman, specializing the in operation and maintenance of Tactical Tomahawk Weapons Control Systems (TTWCS) and Harpoon Weapon Systems (HWS).

**Research Interests:** machine learning, data analytics, data visualization, web science.
